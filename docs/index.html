    Official visual and collision meshes, nominal joint geometry and limits. Source graphical documentation is licensed for visualization; this is not manufacturing CAD.

    Source manifest ↗

    Manufacturer nominal: 20 kg payload · 1.75 m reach · 64 kg arm. Source inertia totals may differ; neither specifies a safe mounted load.

    Original adapter + Robotiq 2F85

    Source gripper joint graph with mimic motion and licensed coupling geometry. The original adapter connects nominal ISO80 and ISO50 patterns.

    The adapter, bolt engagement, coupling and end-tool loads are unqualified. Catalogue workpiece rating does not validate this assembly.

    Body, wheels and suspension

    Original parametric CAD includes a faceted body, removable service panels, six independent corner mounts and source-backed Jantsa / Trelleborg wheel dimensions.

    Tire profile, tread and hidden rim details are original assumptions. The motor exterior is an original dimensional representation; proprietary internals are omitted. Retained bearings, rod ends, guide liners and spring supports are modeled. Wheel/nut application, source profiles, motor-side plugs, controls and loads still need verification.

    Electrical connections

    Two battery modules, isolators, BMS, six motor-controller interfaces and retained breakout boxes are shown. A supported positive/negative cable pair now runs from the main isolator to the BMS, through retained partition glands. Upstream battery wiring, conductor terminations, protection coordination and motor plugs are unfinished. The two UR controller cases still need verified mounts.
